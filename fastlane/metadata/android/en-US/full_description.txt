Link QR Wallet saves your web links on your phone and shows each one as a QR code. A QR code is a square picture that a phone camera can read to open a link.

To add a link, type or paste a web address, scan a QR code, or send a link to the app from another app. If you leave the title empty, the app looks up the page's title after you save, and you can change it. You can search your saved links by title, web address or website name, sort them by title, website or date added, open or copy a link, and share its QR code as a picture.

Your saved links are stored on your phone, so you can search them and show their QR codes without internet. Looking up a page title needs internet.

The app has no account, no ads and no tracking. The camera is used only while you scan a QR code, and the picture is read on your phone and never saved or sent. When you save a link and have not typed a title, the app opens that web address once to read the page's title. Nothing is sent to any website before you save. The website can see your phone's IP address (the number that identifies your connection on the internet). If Android backup is turned on, Android may include your saved links in your own backup.
