Android Link QR Wallet

Save links as QR codes.

Link QR Wallet saves your web links on your phone and shows each one as a QR code. Type or paste a web address, or scan a QR code. After you save, the app looks up the page title for you. Search, sort and share your saved links, and browse them without internet.

What it does

Save a web address and see its QR code straight away. Open the link later with one tap. Search by title, web address or website name, sort your library, and share a QR code as a picture.

Security & privacy

Your saved links stay on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no tracking. It uses the camera only to scan QR codes, and the internet only to look up page titles. When you save a link and have not typed a title, the app looks up the page's title in the background. Nothing is sent to any website before you save. The website can see your phone's IP address. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include your saved links in your own backup. Read the privacy policy.
